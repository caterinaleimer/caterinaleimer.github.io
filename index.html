About

Mathematics for understanding brain dynamics.

I am a PhD researcher at the Department of Mathematics of Politecnico di Milano and a member of the MOX Laboratory.

My work lies at the intersection of numerical analysis, computational neuroscience and scientific computing.

I am particularly interested in accurate and efficient simulations of physiological and pathological neuronal activity, including epilepsy and cerebral ischemia.

I also study multiphysics couplings between electrophysiology, cellular metabolism and the spreading of pathological proteins.

Research interests

From neuronal activity to brain multiphysics.

01

Brain Electrophysiology

Mathematical and numerical modelling of neuronal electrical activity, with emphasis on physiological propagation and pathological events such as epileptic seizures.

↗
02

Electro-metabolic Coupling

Coupled models connecting membrane potentials, ion concentrations, oxygen, ATP dynamics and tissue swelling in cerebral ischemia.

↗
03

Protein Spreading

Investigation of the interaction between abnormal electrophysiological activity and the spreading of pathological proteins in the brain.

↗
04

Advanced Numerical Methods

High-order discontinuous Galerkin methods on polytopal meshes, adaptive strategies and domain decomposition techniques for large-scale simulations.
