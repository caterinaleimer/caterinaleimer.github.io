About

Mathematics for understanding brain dynamics.

I am a PhD student at the Department of Mathematics of Politecnico di Milano and a member of the MOX Laboratory.

My work lies at the intersection of numerical analysis, computational neuroscience and scientific computing.

I am particularly interested in accurate and efficient simulations of physiological and pathological neuronal activity, including epilepsy and cerebral ischemia.

I also study multiphysics couplings between electrophysiology, cellular metabolism and the spreading of pathological proteins.

New publication · June 2026

lymph 2.0 is out now

23 June 2026

Our latest preprint, “The lymph 2.0 library: p-adaptive algorithms and parallel assembly strategies for polytopal DG methods” , is now available on arXiv.

This work presents a new release of the lymph 2.0 library, an open-source MATLAB framework for high-order discontinuous Galerkin discretizations on general polytopal meshes.

The new version supports element-wise polynomial approximation degrees, enabling the construction of p-adaptive strategies based on a posteriori error indicators.

It also introduces a unified assembly framework and dedicated parallel tools for shared-memory computations, improving code modularity, maintainability and computational efficiency.
