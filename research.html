Research area · 02

Brain Electrophysiology

Mathematical and computational modelling of neuronal electrical activity.

Mathematical models provide a powerful framework for describing the propagation of electrical activity through brain tissue and investigating the mechanisms underlying neuronal dynamics.

My research considers both physiological and pathological electrical activity, with particular interest in the onset and propagation of epileptic seizures.

Focus
Neuronal electrical activity
Applications
Epilepsy and seizure propagation
Scale
Brain tissue
Approach
Mathematical and numerical modelling

Research area · 03

Electro-metabolic Coupling

Connecting neuronal electrophysiology with cellular metabolism.

This research investigates the interaction between neuronal electrophysiology and metabolic processes through coupled mathematical models.

The models connect membrane potentials and ionic concentrations with oxygen availability, ATP dynamics and tissue swelling, providing a framework for studying cerebral ischemia.

Focus
Electrophysiology–metabolism coupling
Variables
Ions, oxygen and ATP
Applications
Cerebral ischemia
Phenomena
Electrical dysfunction and tissue swelling

Research area · 04

Protein Spreading

Interaction between brain electrical activity and pathological protein dynamics.

Pathological proteins can spread through brain tissue over long spatial and temporal scales, contributing to the progression of neurological disorders.

My research investigates coupled models describing the interaction between protein spreading and alterations of neuronal electrophysiological activity.

Focus
Pathological protein spreading
Coupling
Protein dynamics and electrophysiology
Scale
Brain tissue
Approach
Coupled mathematical models

Research area · 05

Advanced Numerical Methods

Efficient high-order methods for complex multiphysics problems.

Complex brain models require accurate and computationally efficient numerical methods capable of handling heterogeneous geometries, multiple physical processes and demanding simulations.

My research focuses on high-order discontinuous Galerkin methods on general polytopal meshes, adaptive approximation strategies and domain decomposition techniques.

Methods
Discontinuous Galerkin
Meshes
General polytopal meshes
Adaptivity
High-order adaptive strategies
Solvers
Domain decomposition methods
